## Suggested learning order

1. **Start here:** GitHub/code tools, diagrams, Linux and basic networking.
2. **Build engineering skills:** Python, network automation, Ansible and Terraform.
3. **Practice:** labs, packet analysis, BGP, routing and Internet measurements.
4. **Add security:** cybersecurity labs, threat intelligence, DNS/TLS and exposure discovery.
5. **Expand:** SQL, cloud, observability, IPv6, Git/policy and wireless.
